### Responsible Disclosure Policy

At Nexora, we prioritize security and continuously work to safeguard our systems. If you identify a security issue, please report it so we can address it promptly.

### Scope

-   Public Nexora repositories at [https://github.com/ismailubts/Nexora](https://github.com/ismailubts/Nexora)

### Reporting Guidelines

-   Submit your findings through the repository issue tracker or contact the maintainer directly.
-   Provide clear details to help us reproduce and fix the issue quickly.
-   Ensure that the vulnerability is exploitable. Theoretical or static application security testing reports may be dismissed.
-   Submit the report with CVSS vector and calculated severity when applicable.

### Disclosure Terms

Do not discuss or disclose vulnerability information without prior written consent from Abdul Ismail.
